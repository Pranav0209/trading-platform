# Symbol Universe

This sprint uses a small Indian universe so the numbers are easy to sanity-check and the scope stays narrow enough to support three defensible claims.

| Symbol | Exchange | Reason for inclusion |
|---|---|---|
| `INFY.NS` | NSE | Large-cap IT name, easy to recognise, and already covered by the provided fixtures. Good baseline for checking daily candle movement in rupees. |
| `RELIANCE.NS` | NSE | Broad-market benchmark stock with very liquid trading and familiar price levels. Useful as a second NSE instrument and a contrast to the IT name. |
| `TATASTEEL.BO` | BSE | Gives BSE coverage as required and adds a more cyclical industrial name, which broadens the story without making the universe large. |

The universe stays at three symbols on purpose. That is enough to produce three claims, but small enough that the team can inspect the raw candles, the cleaned frame, and the resulting charts without losing time to scope.
